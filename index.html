ClearFeed, know the trick before you fall for it.

ClearFeed is the app that helps you identify manipulative marketing tactics before they clutter your feed and mind. We aim to educate people susceptible to falling for these tricks through interactive games and relevant blogs! By the end of this experience, you'll be able to clearly define what manipulative marketing is and how to identify it in action. Know the trick before you fall for it.

This app wouldn't be possible without the brilliant minds who helped craft this idea at YYGS: Nihal Odar, Ayoola Odomo, and Suhaani Lohia. As a homage to our YYGS capstone project, we wanted to bring our vision to reality. We hope you enjoy!
-Ayesha and Theo
